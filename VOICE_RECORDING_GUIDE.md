# Sample Voice Recording Script

Use this script when creating voice samples for cloning. Aim for 1-5 minutes of clean, natural speech.

## Recording Tips

1. **Environment**
   - Record in a quiet room
   - Use a good quality microphone
   - Minimize background noise
   - Avoid echo or reverb

2. **Speaking**
   - Speak naturally and clearly
   - Maintain consistent volume
   - Use varied intonation
   - Include different emotions

3. **Technical**
   - Record in WAV format (recommended)
   - Use 24kHz or higher sample rate
   - Mono channel
   - 16-bit or 24-bit depth

## English Sample Text (2-3 minutes)

"Hello, my name is [Your Name], and I'm creating a voice sample for text-to-speech synthesis.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This sentence contains every letter of the alphabet, which helps capture the full range of sounds in my voice.

Let me tell you a short story. Once upon a time, there was a young developer who wanted to build an amazing application. They worked day and night, facing challenges and solving problems. In the end, they succeeded and created something truly wonderful.

Technology is advancing rapidly. Artificial intelligence and machine learning are transforming how we interact with computers. Voice synthesis has come a long way, from robotic monotone to natural, expressive speech.

I can speak with different emotions. When I'm happy, my voice lifts up with excitement! When I'm serious, my tone becomes more measured and deliberate. When I'm asking a question, my voice rises at the end? And when I'm making a statement, it remains steady and confident.

Numbers are important too: one, two, three, four, five. The year is 2024. My phone number is 555-0123. The price is $99.99. It's 3:45 PM.

Thank you for listening to my voice sample. I hope it captures the essence of my natural speech patterns and will be useful for creating a realistic voice clone."

## Recording Checklist

- [ ] Quiet environment with minimal background noise
- [ ] Good quality microphone (or phone in a quiet space)
- [ ] Recorded in WAV or high-quality MP3
- [ ] Natural speaking pace
- [ ] Clear pronunciation
- [ ] Varied emotional expressions
- [ ] Between 1-5 minutes total duration
- [ ] No long pauses or silence
- [ ] Consistent volume throughout
- [ ] No clipping or distortion

## File Preparation

After recording:
1. Save as WAV format (if not already)
2. Trim any silence at start/end
3. Normalize the volume (optional)
4. Ensure file is between 1-5 minutes
5. Upload to Talka via the Voices page

Happy recording!
